I'm **Aravind Anilraj** (`phi5ic`). I'm a systems engineer and Linux contributor currently building **[Nifsi](https://github.com/phi5ic/Nifsi)**, an open-source distributed userspace operating system layer designed for multi-node Linux environments.

### Research & Interests

- **Storage Architecture & Coding Theory:** Erasure coding, MDS array codes (EVENODD, RDP, Liberation, STAR), slice-level regeneration, and write-amplification minimization.
- **Distributed Computing:** Virtual consensus meshes, decentralized state replication, and fast fault recovery.
- **Operating Systems & Linux:** Linux kernel contributions, userspace subsystems, and systems performance engineering.
- **Computer Arithmetic:** Hot-path SIMD optimizations, finite-field arithmetic over GF($2^m$), and hardware-accelerated crypto.

### Projects

- **[Nifsi](https://github.com/phi5ic/Nifsi):** A distributed userspace OS layer on Linux.
- **[StabiSense](https://github.com/phi5ic/StabiSense):** Myosa 5.0 finalist project, dynamically stabilizes chipsets during manufacturing, presented in IEEE Thailand Biosensors Conference (2026).
- **[Qgis-Simulation](https://github.com/phi5ic/Qgis-Simulation):** a high-performance spatial analysis tool designed to identify water pipeline breaches by combining Machine Learning, Dijkstra’s Pathfinding, and Copernicus SAR Imagery. Won first place at the Flowvison Hackathon under Brahma26 (ASIET).
- **Technical Writing:** Rigorous benchmarks, architecture postmortems, and engineering deep dives.
